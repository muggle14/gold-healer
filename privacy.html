Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The Cosmic Alchemy ("we", "us", Garima) respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, what information we collect and how it is used. This is a small, solo spiritual practice — we keep data handling minimal and intentional.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you choose to share when you contact us by WhatsApp, telephone, email, or Instagram, such as your name, contact details, and message.
  • Session details you voluntarily provide for a booking, such as your intention and information relevant to the reading or healing you select.

This website is a static page. It does not use first-party analytics, advertising pixels, or advertising cookies. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive standard technical request data such as your IP address and browser information. Website icons are served locally.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange your session.
  • To prepare and deliver the reading or healing you have booked.

Confidentiality

What you share within a session, or in preparation for one, is held with care and complete confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Forms, WhatsApp & email

The website contact form opens your own email app with a pre-filled message; nothing is sent by the website until you choose to send it. WhatsApp, email, telephone, and Instagram enquiries are handled through those providers, and booking or payment details are arranged directly with you.
